Privacy policy — copias-pve

Last updated: 7 October 2026

copias-pve is a personal backup tool used only by its owner, Pedro, with his own Google account. This policy explains what it does with Google user data.

Data it accesses

It requests a single Google permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, the tool can create files in the owner's Google Drive and read, update or delete only the files it created. It cannot see, read or list any other file in the Drive, and it requests no other Google data (no email, contacts, calendar or profile).

How the data is used

The only use is to store and later restore encrypted backups of the owner's home server. The backups are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload, with a key that is kept only by the owner and is never sent to Google or to anyone else.

Sharing, storage and retention

  • No data is sold, shared or transferred to any third party.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or AI training of any kind.
  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted.
  • Backups are kept in the owner's Drive according to his retention rules and can be removed at any time by deleting the folder or revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

copias-pve's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/jpedrocr/jpedrocr.github.io, or use the developer contact shown on the app's Google consent screen.

Em português

O copias-pve é uma ferramenta pessoal de cópias de segurança, usada só pelo dono (o Pedro), na sua conta Google.
